Stacking on Stacks pays you in bitcoin. Your STX stays locked in your own wallet — what you are choosing is which signer it backs, and a signer is a smart contract. Signer Guide shows you what those contracts do, in plain language, and lets you stake through one from your phone.

WHAT IT OPENS ON

The number you actually want: what a staked STX is earning right now, in sats per 1,000 STX, per payout. Under it, the same figure said three other ways — compounded over a year, what the last completed payout really paid, and how long until the next one — because an estimate is only worth having next to the facts it was built from.

Then your position: how much you have staked, what it earns at that rate, which pool it is with, until when, and where the rewards go.

TWO SCREENS TO YOUR FIRST STAKE

Choose a wallet, say how much. Everything else is already set and shown to you: which pool, why that one, that rewards are held as sBTC so there is no bitcoin address to mistype, and that the lock is one cycle — about two weeks — and can be ended early, without penalty, at the close of the cycle.

Every one of those can be changed. The long way round is still there, and it is the right way if you want it.

WHAT THE POOLS ACTUALLY RUN

Forty-five signer contracts are deployed on pox-5. They run six distinct pieces of code between them. A pool is identified here by what its code hashes to, not by what it is called — anyone can deploy a contract named "signer-manager".

So the guide leads with the contracts: what each one does, whether it can pay rewards to a bitcoin address at all, whether the fee is capped by the code, whether it takes a stake from anyone. Then the pools that run it, with how much is staked behind each and what it charges. Then every pool, ranked by size, searchable.

Backing a different signer later is one transaction, and it takes effect at the next cycle — nothing unlocks in between.

NOTHING IS HIDDEN, INCLUDING WHO WROTE IT

Made by Fast Pool, which runs some of the pools listed here. They are described by the same detectors and ranked by the same rules as everyone else's, and the app says so. The pool it offers a first-time staker is chosen by a rule written down in the source and stated on the screen: a contract that has been read, open to anyone, lowest fee.

Where every number comes from is a screen of its own — which file, when it was generated, which node was asked.

YOUR KEYS STAY IN YOUR WALLET

Signing happens in Leather, Xverse or OKX. Your wallet builds, signs and broadcasts every transaction. This app never sees a private key and never asks for one. It has no accounts, no sign-up, and collects nothing about you.

You can also just watch an address — see what any position holds and earns, without connecting anything.
